==================================================

WHAT DEVWORK IS

==================================================

DevWork is a technical workforce and engineering capacity platform.

DevWork connects individuals, business owners, startups, organizations, and enterprise companies with verified developers, engineers, IT specialists, security professionals, technical consultants, agencies, and technical teams.

The customer does not need to know exactly what kind of technical professional they need.

They describe the problem or desired outcome.

DevWork analyzes the request, determines the required skills, complexity and risk, then routes the work to the appropriate technical provider.

DevWork is NOT simply a freelancer directory.

The core model is:

Customer submits need

→ DevWork analyzes it

→ DevWork determines scope/skills/risk

→ DevWork routes it

→ Provider performs the work

→ DevWork experts provide oversight where appropriate

→ Work is tested/verified

→ Customer reviews and approves

→ Continued support can be purchased if needed

The core positioning is:

“Technical problems.

Solved, not staffed.”

Supporting message:

“You describe what you need done.

DevWork routes, manages, and delivers the right technical expertise—fast.”

DEVWORK WORKFORCE STRUCTURE

==================================================

The system has two important technical workforce layers.

MARKETPLACE PROVIDERS

Independent developers, engineers, agencies, consultants, and specialists.

They perform much of the standard day-to-day execution.

EMPLOYEE EXPERTS

DevWork’s internal senior technical professionals.

Employee Experts are responsible for:

- Technical standards

- Quality control

- Reviews

- Escalations

- Security oversight

- Provider verification

- Enterprise support

- Difficult cases

- Project recovery

The normal logic is:

Marketplace Provider executes

→ DevWork Employee Expert oversees/reviews where appropriate

→ DevWork maintains the customer relationship and delivery standard

High-risk or highly specialized work may be handled directly by Employee Experts, verified specialists, or approved teams.

Do not expose unnecessary internal staffing mechanics everywhere in the marketing site. Explain it where it creates customer trust.
